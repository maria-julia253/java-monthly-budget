# java-monthly-budget

> About the Project: 

  Java program that calculates a user's monthly budget based on their total income and expenses.

> Featured technologies:
	- Java;
	- Arrays;
	- Scanner;
	- Conditional Structures;
	- Repetition Structures.

> Features:
	- Registering the amount of income sources;
	- Registering the amount of expenses;
	- Calculating the amount of income sources;
	- Calculating the amount of expenses;
	- Identifying the budget situation:
		1. Positive budget;
		2. Negative Budget;
		3. Balanced budget.

> Educational goal:

   Training of conditional and repetition structures in Java.
